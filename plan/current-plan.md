# Current plan (transcribed from SEA.pdf)

Trip: Southeast Asia, December–January, most likely 15–16 nights total.
Base split: 2–3 KL · 5 Bangkok · 4 Chiang Mai · 4 Siem Reap (Chiang Rai dropped) = 15–16 nights. The day-by-day sections below are the most accurate version of the plan.

Traveler preferences: peaceful vibes, a sense of adventure, historical & cultural marvels,
impressive skylines and historical sights, soaking up the vibes of a place. Has done a lot of
research already; prefers suggestions that are not at the very top of tourist lists.

## Kuala Lumpur
- KL1: train in from airport; Perdana Botanical Gardens; Petaling Market & Chinatown; Merdeka Square; Merdeka 118 sky view; Central Market. Optional: KLCC at night / hotel pool
- KL2: hotel sunrise; Batu Caves; Saloma Link + Kampung Baru; Petronas & KLCC Park; sunset at Changkat Tunku. Optional: Titiwangsa Lake Gardens
- KL3: Thean Hou Temple; Putrajaya (Putra Mosque, Dataran Putra, Perdana Putra, Persiaran Perdana, Palace of Justice, Tuanku Mizan Zainal Abidin "Iron" Mosque); KL Tower; rooftop infinity pool at night. Optional: FRIM Skywalk, Islamic Arts Museum, photo of KL + Genting
- Already ruled out this trip: Genting Highlands, Malacca, Ipoh

## Bangkok
- BKK1: National Museum; Grand Palace & Emerald Buddha; Wat Pho; Chinatown; Khao San Road at night
- BKK2: sunrise at Wat Arun; Khlong Bang Luang floating market; canals to Wat Paknam; Saphan Taksin; river walk to Asiatique; Chatuchak (night). Optional: Khlong Lat Mayom, Taling Chan floating markets
- BKK3: Ayutthaya day trip by train (Wat Mahathat, Wat Phra Si Sanphet, Wat Chaiwatthanaram, bike around)
- BKK4: slow day; Lumphini Park; Benjakitti Park via skywalk; Sri Maha Mariamman Temple; CBD; sunset at Mahanakhon SkyWalk. Optional: CentralWorld
- BKK5: Ancient City (Muang Boran); Bang Pu recreation center; Wat Ratchanatdaram (Loha Prasat); Golden Mount sunset
- Already ruled out this trip: Maeklong Railway Market, Amphawa, Pattaya, Jim Thompson House, Wat Sam Phran

## Chiang Mai
- CM1: walk the old town; temples, monuments, street food; Silver Temple (Wat Sri Suphan)
- CM2: Coconut Market; Changthong Heritage Park; elephant half-day trip
- CM3: full Doi Suthep day, Monk's Trail (Wat Pha Lat) -> temples & viewpoints -> summit, camp at peak
- CM4: sunrise atop Doi Suthep; Wat Phra That Doi Kham; Royal Park Rajapruek; bike to Wat Umong and Wat Suan Dok
- Already ruled out this trip: Doi Inthanon, Pai, Wat Phra That Hariphunchai (Lamphun), Wat Chaloem Phra Kiat (Lampang), Mae Kampong, Wat Tham Chiang Dao

## Chiang Rai
- DROPPED (the "2 Chiang Rai" line was a mistake). Traveler is open to being convinced otherwise about the northern leg.

## Siem Reap
- SR1: arrive midday; buy 3-day Angkor pass; a couple hours in the park; Old Market
- SR2: Angkor Wat sunrise (walking); Banteay Kdei; Ta Prohm; Ta Keo. Optional: Banteay Samre, Pre Rup
- SR3: Angkor National Museum; Kompong Khleang stilt village tour. Optional: countryside cycling
- SR4: bike: Bayon, Phimeanakas, Preah Khan, Neak Poan; Phnom Bakheng sunset

## Logistics findings
- Thailand–Cambodia land border is CLOSED (since 23 Jun 2025; no reopening date as of Nov 2026). Bangkok → Siem Reap must be a flight (~1 h, ~US$85–110+). Avoid areas within 20 km of the border (UK advice). Sources: gov.uk travel advice for Thailand and Cambodia.
